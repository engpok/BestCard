# Best Card Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 2026

Best Card never requires access to your credit card accounts or financial institution login credentials. Best Card respects your privacy. This app is designed to work without collecting personal information or transmitting your data to external servers.

## Information We Collect

Best Card does not require account creation and does not collect personal information.

The app uses the following data locally on your device:

- **Credit Card Rewards** — Credit card reward information you choose to enter.
- **Text Notes** — Notes you create for credit cards.

All entered information is stored on your device.

Best Card does not track your location in the background after the app is closed.

## Data Storage

All recorded data are stored locally on your device.  
Best Card does not upload, transmit, or share your data (you can create backups that you transfer to external devices).

## Data Sharing

Best Card does not:

- Sell data  
- Share data with third parties  
- Use advertising networks  
- Use third-party analytics services  

## Third-Party Services

Best Card does not contain any third-party tracking SDKs.

## Children’s Privacy

Best Card does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
